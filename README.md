# ExcelMart Nigeria LTD: Sales Performance Dashboard (2025)

An interactive Power BI dashboard analysing a full year of sales performance,
built with DAX to track revenue, cost, profit and profit margin across
product categories, sales reps and months.

## Business Questions
- How much revenue and profit did the business generate in 2025?
- Which product categories drive the most revenue?
- Which sales reps perform best, and how do customer-level sales break down?
- How does revenue change from month to month?

## Dashboard Pages
**1. Summary page**
- KPI cards: Total Revenue, Total Profit, Total Cost, Profit Margin %
- Total Revenue by Category
- Total Revenue by Sales Rep
- Monthly Revenue trend
- Product slicer for filtering

**2. Detail page (drill-through)**
- Drill through from a sales rep to the underlying transactions
- Customer, category, product, payment method, revenue, cost and profit
- KPI cards: Total Revenue, Total Profit, Highest Revenue

## Key Insights
- Total revenue: ₦200M | Total profit: ₦31M | Profit margin: 15.61%
- Electronics is the top category at ₦87M, over 43% of total revenue
- Revenue peaked in March (₦21.2M) and was lowest in August (₦10.5M)
- Top sales rep: Yetunde Adeyemi (₦29.8M)

## DAX Measures
- Total Revenue
- Total Cost
- Total Profit
- Profit Margin %

## Tools Used
Power BI Desktop, DAX, Excel (data source)
